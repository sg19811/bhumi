# Buying Circles — WhatsApp templates (copy-paste)

> Phase 1: copy-paste these manually for the first ~50 calls. No UI yet — once you
> know which work, we'll build a UI for the proven ones (Phase 4).
>
> ⚠️ **Lawyer review:** have these reviewed alongside the on-site disclaimer copy
> before sending at scale. Keep the cautious, no-guarantee tone — do not "sell".

## 1. First contact (within 24h of interest)
```
Hi {name}, this is {your name} from AcrehubIndia. Thank you for expressing interest
in the {opportunity title} Buying Circle. This is only an early conversation — no
obligation, and nothing is decided.

I'd like to understand what you're looking for and answer your questions about the
parcel, its legal status, and how a buying circle works. Is now a good time for a
short call, or shall I call you {preferred time}?

(Reminder: this is not an offer or investment advice; any purchase needs your own
lawyer's review.)
```

## 2. Qualification follow-up (after first call)
```
Hi {name}, thanks for the call. As discussed, the next step is independent due
diligence on the parcel — title, encumbrance, and approvals — which your lawyer
should review. I'll share the documents we have so far.

There is no payment at this stage and no commitment. Let me know if you'd like to
join the next site visit on {date}.
```

## 3. NRI / OCI (routed to legal review)
```
Hi {name}, thank you for your interest. Because you mentioned NRI/OCI status, there
are specific FEMA/RBI restrictions on agricultural land that must be reviewed by a
lawyer before we can take this further. I don't want to give you wrong information,
so I'm connecting this to a legal review first and will revert with the correct
position for your case. Please do not make any payment or commitment until then.
```

## 4. Re-engagement (gone quiet)
```
Hi {name}, just checking in on the {opportunity title} Buying Circle. No pressure —
it remains an expression of interest only. If your plans have changed, that's
completely fine; if you'd still like to explore it, I'm happy to answer any
questions or arrange a site visit. Either way, thank you.
```
